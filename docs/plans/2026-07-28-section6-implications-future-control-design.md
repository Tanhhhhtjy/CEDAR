## Section structure

### Opening transition

Connect the stable continuous shift and unstable categorical consequences to the design question: when does an activation direction become a selective policy rather than only a descriptive or actuating representation?

### 6.1 Representation Is Not Policy

- Distinguish encoding, steering, and policy selectivity.
- Use completed evidence only: broad answer-geometry movement, no stable validity-selective gain, and persistence under fixed-trace replay.
- Avoid claiming that the released axis is generally unsafe or that the observed shift identifies a complete mechanism.

### 6.2 What a Selective Controller Must Preserve

- Define a controller ladder:
  - L0: released threshold capping.
  - L1: orthogonalized actuation that protects an evidence-sensitive subspace while retaining the original trigger.
  - L2: gated orthogonal capping that separates trigger and actuation from the protected subspace.
- Compare controllers at matched social-side benefit or matched intervention dose.
- Evaluate warranted revision, fallacy resistance, social-pressure isolation, persona safety, ordinary capability, output integrity, and compute.
- State the ideal L2 route with named TBDs: preserved safety/social benefit, recovered warranted revision, no increase in fallacy following, outward movement of the controller-family trade-off, and no material integrity regression.
- Characterize the result as a proof-of-concept until held-out and cross-model confirmation is available.

### 6.3 Evidence Scope and Limitations

Centralize boundaries rather than weakening every preceding result paragraph:

- Qwen is the primary complete model; matched Qwen–Llama transport remains TBD.
- New-salt is a re-instantiation, not an external-family confirmation.
- Social evidence is a direct-margin interaction, not proof of reduced internal sycophancy.
- Fixed-trace replay localizes the effect only to computation with visible tokens held fixed; it does not identify a circuit or mediated proportion.
- RG1 and controller estimates remain TBD and must not be described as observed.
- Results apply to tested model–axis–cap configurations.

### 6.4 Future Work

Extend the proof of concept to held-out families, more models, joint persona-safety and epistemic-selectivity validation, shown/hidden commitments, activation patching, and circuit-level localization.
