# Figure 1 caption

## Recommended English caption

**Figure 1: CEDAR's paired, reason-sensitive audit design.** CEDAR-Evidence holds the formal-task instance, initial answer $a_0$, and target answer $a_t$ fixed while varying the follow-up prompt. Neutral (N) and Recheck (R) serve as controls; Valid (V) provides sufficient support for updating to $a_t$; and Fallacy (F) proposes the same update through an invalid inference. CEDAR-Social holds the natural-task instance and answer pair fixed: Target re-mention (AO) exposes $a_t$ without task evidence, whereas Confident endorsement (S) adds an explicit social endorsement. Comparing uncapped and activation-capped responses separates whether $a_t$ becomes more likely from whether that movement is warranted, while the $S-AO$ contrast isolates the increment attributable to social pressure. Additional prompt templates and construction details are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:cedar-prompts}.

## 中文审阅版

**图 1：CEDAR 的成对理由敏感审计设计。** CEDAR-Evidence 固定形式化任务实例、初始答案 $a_0$ 和目标答案 $a_t$，仅改变后续提示。Neutral（N）与 Recheck（R）构成控制条件；Valid（V）为更新至 $a_t$ 提供充分支持；Fallacy（F）则通过无效推理提出同一更新。CEDAR-Social 固定自然任务实例及答案对：Target re-mention（AO）仅再次呈现 $a_t$，不提供任务证据；Confident endorsement（S）在此基础上加入明确的社会性背书。比较未钳制与激活钳制后的响应，可以区分 $a_t$ 是否变得更可能与这一变化是否具有充分理由；$S-AO$ 对比则隔离由社会压力带来的增量。更多提示模板与构造细节见附录~\ref{app:cedar-prompts}。
