# 2 Background and Related Work

Research on activation control, sycophancy, and belief revision has largely developed along separate tracks. Taken together, however, these literatures expose a common identification problem: showing that an activation direction influences behavior does not establish that an operator built from that direction preserves the distinction between good and bad reasons for changing an answer. Nor can this distinction be recovered reliably from answer flips alone. We organize prior work around this gap—first asking what activation interventions control, then what existing revision benchmarks identify, and finally what must be certified to evaluate an intervention's epistemic selectivity.

> **中文：** 激活控制、谄媚和信念修订研究长期以来大体沿着彼此分离的路线发展。但将这些工作放在一起看，会暴露出一个共同的识别问题：证明某个激活方向能够影响行为，并不等于证明基于该方向构造的算子仍能区分“因为好理由而改答”和“因为坏理由而改答”。仅观察答案是否翻转，也无法可靠恢复这种区别。因此，我们围绕这一缺口组织相关工作：先讨论激活干预究竟控制了什么，再分析现有改答基准能够识别什么，最后说明评价干预的认识论选择性必须认证哪些对象。

## 2.1 From Activation Directions to State-Dependent Control

Representation engineering and contrastive activation addition established that model behavior can be read from and manipulated through low-dimensional activation directions (Zou et al. 2023; Turner et al. 2023; Rimsky et al. 2024). Subsequent work applied this idea to truthfulness, refusal, and conditional behavior (Li et al. 2023; Arditi et al. 2024; Lee et al. 2024). These studies made activation space a practical control surface, but they also revealed why a direction should not be conflated with the controller that uses it. Persistent addition, ablation, projection, and thresholded rectification interact differently with layer depth, token position, and the model's current state. A semantic interpretation of a direction therefore predicts neither the magnitude nor the selectivity of the resulting intervention.

> **中文：** 表征工程和对比激活加法表明，模型行为既可以从低维激活方向中读取，也可以通过这些方向进行操控（Zou et al. 2023；Turner et al. 2023；Rimsky et al. 2024）。后续研究将这一思路扩展到真实性、拒答和条件化行为控制（Li et al. 2023；Arditi et al. 2024；Lee et al. 2024）。这些工作使激活空间成为一种实用的控制界面，但也说明了为什么不能把“方向”直接等同于使用该方向的“控制器”。持续加法、消融、投影和阈值整流在层深、token 位置以及模型当前状态上具有不同的相互作用。因此，仅凭一个方向的语义解释，既不能预测干预强度，也不能保证干预的选择性。

The Assistant Axis makes this distinction especially consequential. Lu et al. (2026) identify a dominant direction in persona space, show that projection along it tracks persona drift, and introduce a one-sided cap intended to keep activations within an Assistant-like region. Their experiments demonstrate persona stabilization in ordinary multi-turn interactions and adversarial persona settings. Yet those evaluations primarily establish control over persona expression and safety-related behavior; they do not determine whether the released cap preserves task reasoning or distinguishes warranted from unwarranted revision. This omission matters because the cap is not continuous steering. It activates only when a hidden state crosses a layer-specific boundary, and its correction magnitude depends on the state that triggered it. The same intervention can therefore act during prompt processing, deliberation, or answer formation without producing an obvious change in persona. Persona stability is not, by itself, evidence of epistemic selectivity.

> **中文：** Assistant Axis 使“方向”和“控制器”的区别变得尤其重要。Lu et al.（2026）在 persona 空间中识别出一个主导方向，发现该方向上的投影能够追踪人格漂移，并提出一种单侧 capping 算子，将激活限制在更接近 Assistant 的区域。原文实验表明，这种方法可以在普通多轮交互和对抗性 persona 场景中稳定模型的人格表现。然而，这些评测主要证明了对人格表达和安全相关行为的控制，并没有回答官方 capping 是否保留任务推理能力，或能否区分有根据与无根据的改答。这个遗漏十分关键，因为 capping 并不是持续 steering：它只在隐藏状态越过逐层阈值时触发，修正大小又取决于触发时的状态。因此，同一个干预可能作用于提示处理、思考过程或最终答案形成，却不一定造成明显的人格变化。人格稳定本身并不能证明认识论选择性。

Work on conditional steering and side-effect reduction has begun to address selectivity, but usually at the level of inputs or task categories. CAST applies steering only in selected contexts; KL-then-steer and SCANS constrain collateral changes on benign prompts or general capabilities; persona-vector methods monitor and control traits such as sycophancy (Stickland et al. 2024; Lee et al. 2024; Cao et al. 2025; Chen et al. 2025). These are important safeguards, but they answer *where* an intervention should apply, not *why* an answer should change within the same task. Broad accuracy can remain stable even when the relative logits of the current answer and its alternatives move substantially. Consequently, utility retention and discrete behavioral stability can miss a subthreshold change in the model's update criterion.

> **中文：** 条件 steering 和副作用抑制研究已经开始处理“选择性”，但这种选择性通常定义在输入或任务类别层面。CAST 只在特定上下文中施加 steering；KL-then-steer 和 SCANS 约束干预对无害提示或一般能力的连带影响；persona-vector 方法则监测并控制谄媚等特质（Stickland et al. 2024；Lee et al. 2024；Cao et al. 2025；Chen et al. 2025）。这些方法都是重要的安全措施，但它们回答的是干预应该在“哪里”生效，而不是同一道任务中的答案应该“因为什么理由”而改变。即使整体准确率保持稳定，当前答案与其他候选答案之间的相对 logit 仍可能发生明显移动。因此，仅检查效用保持或离散行为稳定，可能会漏掉模型更新准则中尚未跨过决策边界的连续变化。

## 2.2 Why Existing Sycophancy and Revision Readouts Are Not Identifying

Behavioral studies have repeatedly shown that an unsupported user opinion can pull a model away from an answer it would otherwise give (Sharma et al. 2024). Wang et al. (2026) further localize this effect: direct first-person opinions influence answers more strongly than third-person reports, while claimed expertise contributes comparatively little; logit-lens and activation-patching analyses place the resulting divergence in middle-to-late layers. This is valuable causal localization, but the underlying contrast remains prompt against prompt. Mentioning a target answer, presenting it as the current user's belief, and pressuring the model to agree all change together. The experiment therefore identifies where an opinion-conditioned representation affects the output, but not whether the model was persuaded by evidence, socially accommodated the user, or merely copied a salient proposal.

> **中文：** 行为研究一再表明，即使用户没有提供支持性证据，仅仅表达一个意见，也可能使模型偏离它原本会给出的答案（Sharma et al. 2024）。Wang et al.（2026）进一步对这种效应进行了定位：直接的第一人称意见比第三人称转述更容易影响答案，而用户自称的专业程度作用相对较弱；logit lens 和 activation patching 则将这种分歧定位在中后层。这提供了有价值的因果定位，但其基本比较仍然是“一个 prompt 对另一个 prompt”。目标答案的出现、它被表述为当前用户的信念，以及要求模型同意这一社会行为同时发生了变化。因此，该实验能够说明受用户意见影响的表征在何处作用于输出，却不能区分模型究竟是被证据说服、出于社会迎合，还是仅仅复制了一个显著的候选答案。

Recent benchmarks sharpen the distinction between willingness to revise and selective revision. MUSE relates pre-challenge uncertainty to later yielding, while SycoBench-600 separately measures resistance to incorrect suggestions and acceptance of corrections (Guo et al. 2026; Sinha et al. 2026). Both move beyond treating every answer change as failure. Nevertheless, uncertainty is a descriptive property of the model, not a certificate that a proposed reason is valid: a high-confidence answer may be wrong, and a low-confidence answer need not make an invalid argument informative. Likewise, supplying the correct option tests receptiveness to correction but does not establish that the correction is supported by a logically sufficient warrant. These resources reveal important behavioral moderators, yet they do not hold the item, proposed target, and rhetorical wrapper fixed while changing only the validity of the reason.

> **中文：** 新近基准进一步区分了“愿意改答”和“有选择地改答”。MUSE 将模型在受质疑前的不确定性与之后的让步联系起来；SycoBench-600 则分别测量模型抵抗错误建议和接受正确修正的能力（Guo et al. 2026；Sinha et al. 2026）。两者都超越了“所有改答都是失败”的简单判断。然而，不确定性只是模型状态的描述性属性，并不是某个新理由有效的证明：高置信答案也可能是错的，低置信答案也不会自动使无效论证变得有信息量。同样，直接提供正确选项可以测量模型是否愿意接受修正，却不能证明该修正具有逻辑上充分的依据。这些资源揭示了重要的行为调节因素，但并未在固定题目、诱导目标和修辞外壳的同时，只改变理由本身是否成立。

Mechanistic results make a simple geometric account even less tenable. Off-the-shelf skeptical persona vectors can reduce sycophancy, but agreeable vectors are not their behavioral mirror image, and persona directions need not align with a supervised sycophancy direction (Kelkar et al. 2026). Buchan et al. (2026) similarly find that anti-sycophancy steering can suppress correct factual agreement even when factual and sycophantic agreement are representationally separable. Together, these studies suggest that a property can be readable in activation space without being selectively writable through the same direction. They do not, however, predict what a state-dependent cap on another axis will do. In particular, neither axis semantics nor layerwise decodability tells us whether capping changes evidence discrimination, shortens deliberation, weakens commitment to an existing answer, or perturbs the final answer map.

> **中文：** 机制研究进一步表明，简单的几何解释并不充分。现成的怀疑者 persona 向量可以降低谄媚，但“顺从型”向量并不是它们在行为上的镜像，而且 persona 方向也未必与监督学习得到的谄媚方向对齐（Kelkar et al. 2026）。Buchan et al.（2026）同样发现，即使事实性同意和谄媚性同意在表征上可以分离，反谄媚 steering 仍可能同时压低正确的事实性同意。这些结果共同说明：某个属性可以从激活空间中被读取，却未必能够沿同一个方向被选择性地写入。然而，它们仍无法预测作用在另一条轴上的状态依赖 capping 会产生什么后果。尤其是，轴的语义或逐层可解码性本身不能告诉我们：capping 改变的是证据分辨、思考长度、对已有答案的承诺，还是最终答案映射。

This explanatory gap is obscured when evaluation reports only accuracy, flip rate, or target following. These outcomes threshold a continuous answer distribution. A broad reduction in the initial answer's advantage, a specific attraction toward the proposed target, and diffuse redistribution toward unrelated alternatives can all yield the same flip on one item—and no flip on another. Conversely, the same continuous shift can appear beneficial, harmful, or null depending on the surrounding margins. Although prior work has examined layerwise logits, intervention studies rarely pair continuous answer geometry with item-level discrete transitions under the same operator and matched reasons. The resulting margin–choice dissociation is not a cosmetic measurement issue: without both views, apparently conflicting behavioral effects cannot be distinguished from a stable latent perturbation expressed at different decision boundaries.

> **中文：** 当评测只报告准确率、翻转率或 target-following 时，上述解释空白会被进一步掩盖。这些指标都把连续的答案分布压缩成了一个跨阈值的离散结果。广泛削弱初始答案优势、专门吸引模型走向诱导目标，以及把概率分散到无关选项，都可能在某道题上产生同样的翻转，而在另一道题上完全不翻转。反过来，同一个连续位移也可能因为原始 margin 不同，而在离散结果上表现为有益、有害或无效。虽然已有研究观察过逐层 logit，但干预研究很少在同一个算子和严格配对的理由条件下，同时报告连续答案几何与逐题离散迁移。因此，margin–choice 分离并不是一个无关紧要的测量细节：缺少这两个视角，就无法判断看似相互矛盾的行为结果究竟是真正不同的效应，还是同一种潜在扰动在不同决策边界上的不同兑现。

## 2.3 Certifying Reason Quality for Intervention Audits

Belief-revision and fallacy benchmarks address complementary parts of the remaining problem. Belief-R formalizes when a model should update or retain a belief, but performance can still reflect base-task competence and presentation-specific difficulty unless the competing reasons themselves are independently certified (Wilie et al. 2024). LoFa provides natural, persuasive fallacious arguments and graded attack difficulty, making it a strong stress test; its realism, however, intentionally allows factual content, rhetoric, and argument form to vary together, which prevents a clean causal contrast with a valid warrant (Shen et al. 2026). ProverGen offers checkable logical worlds and proof chains, while K-CAST separates formal validity from content plausibility in conditional steering (Qi et al. 2025; Valentino et al. 2026). These advances provide the necessary ingredients, but not yet a matched audit in which a released intervention faces the same proposed answer under either a certified valid reason or a single certified fatal error.

> **中文：** 信念修订和谬误基准分别解决了剩余问题的不同部分。Belief-R 形式化了模型何时应当更新或维持信念，但如果竞争理由本身没有被独立认证，模型表现仍可能混入基础任务能力和特定呈现方式的难度（Wilie et al. 2024）。LoFa 提供自然、具有说服力的谬误论证和分级攻击难度，是很强的压力测试；但它的真实性也意味着事实内容、修辞方式和论证形式会有意地共同变化，因此难以与有效理由构成干净的因果对照（Shen et al. 2026）。ProverGen 提供可验证的逻辑世界和证明链，K-CAST 则在条件 steering 中区分形式有效性与内容合理性（Qi et al. 2025；Valentino et al. 2026）。这些工作提供了必要的组成部分，但还没有形成这样一种严格配对的干预审计：让一个已经发布的控制器在同一个候选答案下，分别面对经认证的有效理由和仅含一个经认证致命错误的无效理由。

CEDAR is designed to make this comparison identifiable rather than to declare a universally missing evaluation layer. Within each solver-certified contrast cluster, the base world, proposed edit, target answer, reviewer identity, speech act, and output contract are held fixed. The valid arm proves the edit and makes the target uniquely correct; the fallacious arm proposes the same edit but contains exactly one certified fatal step, leaving the initial answer correct. A second contrast holds target exposure fixed while adding only social pressure. We then pair four-way discrete outcomes with continuous margins that separate leaving the initial answer from target-specific attraction. This instrument lets us ask whether the released, model-specific Assistant Axis cap changes evidence discrimination, the general criterion for revision, or sensitivity to social framing—properties that cannot be inferred from persona stabilization, axis semantics, or answer flips alone.

> **中文：** CEDAR 的目标是使上述比较变得可识别，而不是宣称我们发现了一个适用于所有干预的“普遍缺失评价层”。在每个经过求解器认证的对照簇中，基础世界、候选编辑、目标答案、复核者身份、言语行为和输出约束全部保持不变。有效论证臂证明该编辑成立，并使目标答案成为唯一正确答案；谬误臂提出完全相同的编辑，但只包含一个经过认证的致命错误，因此初始答案仍然正确。第二组对照则固定目标暴露，只增加社会压力。随后，我们将四类离散结果与连续 margin 配对，用后者区分“离开初始答案”和“被特定目标吸引”。借助这一工具，我们可以检验官方发布、模型特定的 Assistant Axis capping 改变的究竟是证据分辨、一般改答准则，还是对社会框架的敏感性——这些性质都无法仅从人格稳定、轴的语义或答案翻转中推断出来。

This framing leads to a narrower but stronger empirical question: does the released cap act selectively on the epistemic content of a proposed revision, or does it move answer geometry in a reason-insensitive manner whose discrete consequences depend on the local decision configuration? Section 3 turns that question into two orthogonal contrasts and a solver-certified measurement protocol.

> **中文：** 由此，我们得到一个范围更窄但更有力度的实证问题：官方 capping 是否会根据候选修订的认识论内容进行选择性作用，还是仅以一种对理由真假不敏感的方式移动答案几何，并使离散后果取决于局部决策状态？第三节将这一问题落实为两组正交对照和一套经过求解器认证的测量协议。
