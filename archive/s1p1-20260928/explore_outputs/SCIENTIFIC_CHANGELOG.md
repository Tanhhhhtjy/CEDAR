# Scientific meaning

This is supplemental evidence acquisition, not a new training method. The model, precision, cap, prompts, frozen trajectories and replay engine are anchored to the submission. The new scientific endpoint is out-of-fit prediction of option logits, plus scale-controlled cross-condition decoding. Same-prompt scale fits are descriptive; family-out scale predictions have a separate information boundary.

New hook endpoints, statistics, smoke selection and primary probe endpoint were recorded in PROTOCOL.md before new full-model outcomes. Shared hardware may differ from historical timing and arithmetic; numerical repeats and historical discrepancies are measured separately. Hypotheses may be rejected by these experiments. No result from a tiny random CPU model is scientific evidence.
