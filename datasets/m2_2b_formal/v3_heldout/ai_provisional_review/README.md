# AI provisional review — not a human-review gate

These files are construction-assisted provisional labels for a fast engineering experiment only.

- `independent_human_review=false`
- `official_human_review_status=not_started`
- The labels use the hidden construction key and author construction audit.
- They must not be used to report raw human agreement, Cohen's kappa, or passage of the dual-human-review gate.
- The two official reviewer templates remain blank.

Included artifacts:

- `M2_2b_v3_AI_临时预审_labels.csv`: machine-readable provisional labels.
- `M2_2b_v3_AI_临时预审_summary.json`: status, counts, and hashes.
- `M2_2b_v3_AI_临时预审_非人工.xlsx`: bilingual inspection workbook with a prominent non-human notice.

The formal dataset remains in the parent directory. According to `PHASE2_1_CODEX_REVIEW.md`, a definitive experiment still requires two saved independent human raw reviews and the human-review gate.
