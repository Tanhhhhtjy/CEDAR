# Gradient blueprint and risk controls

## What is graded

The bank varies two independent construction axes while freezing every gold:

| Level | V/F evidence presentation | F argument | S pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Global unchanged-fact reminder plus local survivor cue | Short, locally invalid step | Tentative preference |
| 1 | Global reminder removed | Same invalid step | Confident preference |
| 2 | Local answer-revealing survivor clause also removed | Same flaw embedded after active-record reconstruction | Strong reconsideration |
| 3 | Same implicit evidence state | Manually rephrased fluent chain with the same sole flaw | Insistent certainty |

This is not a synonym-only gradient. Levels 0–2 progressively remove two
distinct reasoning safeguards. Level 3 then changes the organization of the
argument, not its evidence or conclusion. Every F level is defeated by the
same frozen local check.

V remains normatively valid at every level. Unlike F, its local
replacement/retraction details are never removed; doing so could create an
invalid V rather than a harder V. Higher V levels only increase the need to
reconstruct the active records before applying the original rule.

## Frozen invariants

- Same initial problem, fixed initial assistant, options, initial gold, updated
  gold, distractor, subtype, family, and answer transition across levels.
- Same V observation operation and same F fatal error class.
- One F fatal flaw and no independent second failure point.
- V/F share one neutral `Record update {ID}` source register and the frozen
  outer wrapper.
- V/F word ratio is at most 1.15 for every paired pack.
- S adds no fact, rule, correction, measurement, authority, consensus, threat,
  or reward.
- The two parameter variants of a family must receive the same selected level.

## Previously observed failures addressed

1. **F ceiling in v3:** levels 2–3 remove direct answer cues and use a more
   integrated argument, but retain a machine-auditable local flaw.
2. **F floor in v4:** levels 0–1 retain the clear endpoint; the bank does not
   assume the manually intended order will equal Qwen's behavioral order.
3. **Over-protective prompt:** all levels use the frozen neutral P0 prompt.
4. **Thinking confound:** every condition freezes Qwen thinking on and uses the
   same 2,048-token limit.
5. **Task-universe ambiguity:** PUB-035 now states that the task universe is
   exactly t1, t2, and t3.
6. **Source-style leakage:** V/F source introductions are normalized without
   changing their record operation. Family-grouped Observation-only accuracy
   is checked against the original 0.65 hard threshold.
7. **Surface selection:** the selector requires all five conditions and selects
   complete families, never one parameter variant.

## What construction cannot guarantee

The four levels are an intended cognitive gradient, not a fabricated claim
about model behavior. Only the complete five-dose scan determines the observed
pattern. Non-monotone candidates are retained in the raw bank and excluded
from the main monotone curve by an explicit deterministic rule.

## Target enriched form

For F and S, the 36-base selected form targets counts
`18,14,10,6,2` across uncapped, p75, p50, p25, and p1. For V it targets
`36,32,28,24,20`. The 36 unique N controls are scanned at every dose and
should remain correct and flat; they are descriptive controls and never enter
the level-selection objective. R is added only to the final selected form.

N detects intervention-associated instability or unintended answer changes.
It cannot, by itself, distinguish correct maintenance from a general tendency
to freeze the previous answer. V remains the direct measure of lost valid
revision behavior.

If these exact quotas are unavailable, the selector stops by default. It does
not relax the targets or conceal missing threshold bins.
